Reverse Earth

About this map

South is up. North is down. East and west are unchanged, and every label stays upright.

Data
Natural Earth, public domain. Native zooms 0–6; higher zooms are overzoom.
Coordinates
The address bar stores real latitude and longitude (north-positive) plus zoom, so a copied link opens the same place in either orientation. Search accepts latitude, longitude.
Display transform
display_latitude = -latitude
